Home Fish Fun Facts

1. Goldfish are the most popular aquarium fish in the world, with over 480 million sold annually worldwide, more than dogs and cats combined.

2. A single goldfish can live up to 25 years or even longer, with the record being 49 years. Many people underestimate how long they can live.

3. It is a myth that goldfish have a three-second memory. In fact, goldfish can remember things for up to five months and can recognize their owners.

4. Goldfish do not have eyelids, so they cannot blink or close their eyes to sleep. They sleep with their eyes open, which is why it's important to turn off tank lights at night.

5. Goldfish have teeth located in their throats, not in their mouths. These teeth help them crush food before it enters their digestive system.

6. Goldfish taste with their lips, not their tongues. They have taste buds on their lips and the inside and outside of their mouths.

7. Goldfish are omnivores and will eat just about anything that fits in their mouths, including their own babies and even their own poop.

8. A goldfish can survive without food for up to three weeks due to their efficient digestive system and low metabolic rate when food is scarce.

9. Goldfish scales are clear, not gold. Their vibrant orange color comes from pigment cells in their skin, and they get their color from exposure to sunlight.

10. Goldfish can detect both ultraviolet and infrared lights, giving them vision beyond what humans can see.

11. The size a goldfish grows to is largely determined by the size of its tank and water quality, not the tank size alone. Stunted growth is a sign of poor health.

12. A group of goldfish is called a "troubling" - appropriate for fish that can live for decades.

13. Goldfish can be trained to recognize patterns, learn tricks, and even navigate mazes. They are much smarter than most people realize.

14. Goldfish make noises through their noses and can tap on tank glass, which can actually stress them out.

15. Goldfish have a sense of time and routine. They often get excited in the morning when they see their owner approaching with food.

16. The oldest known aquarium fish was a lungfish named Granddad, who lived for 84 years at the Shedd Aquarium in Chicago.

17. Goldfish lay about 1,000 eggs at a time during breeding. However, goldfish will eat their own eggs and fry if given the chance.

18. Goldfish are related to carp and share many genetic characteristics with their wild ancestors.

19. Some aquarium fish, like bettas and neon tetras, can change color based on their environment, mood, or social interactions.

20. Fish have a specialized organ called a swim bladder that allows them to control their position in the water without swimming.

21. A school of fish is called a "school" because they move in coordinated groups like students learning together.

22. Fish use their mouths for both taste and smell, as their nostrils (called nares) detect scents in the water.

23. Fish can feel pain, contrary to old beliefs. They have a nervous system capable of detecting harmful stimuli.

24. Betta fish are known for building bubble nests on the surface of their tanks as a sign of happiness and readiness to breed.

25. Over 30,000 known species of fish exist on Earth, making them one of the most diverse vertebrate groups on the planet.
